# Roadmap

Planwise is educational software. This roadmap lists what is planned, in rough order, and what will not be built. Dates are not promised.

## Released

**1.2.0** Dashboard, check-in and milestones, bank statement import, goals, debts, prepay-or-invest, net worth, redesign, bundled font and PDF reader, accessibility improvements, tests.

## Next

### 1.3 Bonds and protection
- Compare an FD, a listed bond and a government security after tax, including capital gains rules.
- A step-by-step guide to buying government securities.
- A better insurance check using the human life value method, health super top-up and medical inflation.
- A "what changed" note whenever the tax year changes.

### 1.4 Independence
- An independence number, with Coast and Barista variants.
- Chance of success for the whole plan, not only the small-cap part.
- A bucket plan for early-retirement spending, and healthcare costs.
- Household mode with two incomes.

### 2.0 Reach
- An installable offline version.
- Bengali and Hindi.
- Country packs, so others can add the UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore and the UAE.
- Embeddable widgets.
- A modular build, with a full automatic test suite.

## Will not be built
- **Bank linking or account aggregation.** It would break the privacy promise.
- **Specific investment tips, product rankings or an advice chatbot.** Planwise compares numbers. It does not tell people what to buy.
- **Accounts, ads or analytics.**

## Keeping it responsible
- Defaults stay neutral. Splits, multiples and rates are for the user to choose.
- Tax rules are versioned by year and clearly dated.
- Before promoting Planwise widely, please get legal advice on local rules for financial planning tools, such as SEBI's rules in India.
