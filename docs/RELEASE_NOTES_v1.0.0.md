## Planwise 1.0.0

The first public release of Planwise, an open-source planner for your emergency fund, steady income and big purchases. It runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no server.

### What's included
- **Emergency fund**: Basic, Advanced and Custom calculators, where-to-keep tiers and an insurance check
- **Bond ladder** with a small-cap fund and a chance-of-success simulation
- **5-year plan** from a salary slip, with a stress test
- **Purchases** planner
- **Calendar** with day details and a calendar file (.ics)
- **Scenarios** side by side
- **Tax rules** for India (old and new regime), the United States, Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands, all editable
- **Currency** selector: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, CAD, AUD
- **Explain it simply**: a plain-words guide to every topic
- **Save, load, share and print**

### Files in this release
- `Planwise-User-Guide.pdf`: a 19-page guide to every tool
- `Planwise-Brochure.pdf`: a two-sided overview
- `planwise-demo.mp4`: a 1-minute captioned tour

### Good to know
- Tax defaults were last checked in November 2026. Please check them against official sources.
- Planwise gives educational estimates. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.
