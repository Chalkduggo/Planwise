## Planwise 1.2.0

The first public release of Planwise, an open-source planner for your emergency fund, steady income, goals and debts. It runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no server.

### Highlights
- **Dashboard:** a live view of your plan, milestones, goals and debts
- **Check-in:** compare what really happened with your plan every month, and build a streak
- **Goals, Debts and Net worth:** save for the future, pay debts faster, and see what you own minus what you owe
- **Bank statement import:** read a CSV on your device and use real averages as your expenses
- **Prepay or invest:** compare paying a loan early with investing
- **Emergency fund, bond ladder, 5-year plan, purchases, calendar and scenarios**
- **Tax rules** for India (old and new regime), the United States, Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands, all editable
- **Currency** selector: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, CAD, AUD
- **Explain it simply:** a plain-words guide to every topic

### Privacy
Nothing you type leaves your browser. The font is built in and the PDF reader is bundled, so Planwise does not load anything from other sites.

### Files in this release
- `Planwise-User-Guide.pdf`: a 28-page guide to every tool
- `Planwise-Brochure.pdf`: a two-sided overview
- `planwise-demo.mp4`: a captioned tour of about a minute and a half

### Good to know
- Tax defaults were last checked in November 2026. Please check them against official sources.
- Planwise gives educational estimates. It is not financial, tax or investment advice.
