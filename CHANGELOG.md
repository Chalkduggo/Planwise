# Changelog

All notable changes to Planwise are listed here.

## [1.0.0] - November 2026

First public release.

### Added
- Emergency fund calculator: Basic, Advanced and Custom salary path, where-to-keep tiers, insurance check.
- Bond ladder with small-cap fund and a chance-of-success simulation.
- 5-year plan with salary slip reading, tax estimate, other income, expenses and a stress test.
- Purchases planner.
- Calendar with day details and a calendar file (.ics).
- Scenarios for side-by-side comparison.
- Tax rules for India (old and new regime), the United States, Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands. All editable.
- Currency selector: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, CAD, AUD.
- "Explain it simply" plain-words guide.
- Save, load, share and print.
- User guide, brochure and demo video.
